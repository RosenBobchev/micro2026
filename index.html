3 · Predator–prey transfer: biodilution or bioaccumulation?

BMF = median MP concentration in Rapana ÷ median MP concentration in Mytilus, calculated separately for each station.

BMF < 1 lower concentration in predator than prey · BMF > 1 higher concentration in predator than prey. A BMF > 1 is shown here as apparent trophic bioaccumulation, not proof of causal biomagnification.
